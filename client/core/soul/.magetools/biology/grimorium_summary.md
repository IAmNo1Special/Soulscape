# Domains
Reproductive Biology Simulation, Kinship Systems Modeling, Biological Entity Management.

# Summary
This suite of tools provides core functionalities for simulating fundamental aspects of biological entity management within a defined system. Capabilities include the programmatic creation of new biological individuals (offspring), the establishment and formalization of parent-offspring kinship relationships, and the assignment of unique identifiers or names to these entities. All operations are designed with conditional logic, particularly sensitive to gender-based biological parameters for processes such as reproduction and relationship establishment.

# Major Capabilities
- **Biological Entity Generation**: Programmatically simulates the creation of new individual entities (offspring) contingent upon specific biological prerequisites, such as gender compatibility.
- **Kinship Relationship Formalization**: Establishes and records parent-offspring relationships between biological entities, incorporating gender as a determinant for relationship validity.
- **Individual Identifier Assignment**: Assigns unique names or identifiers to biological entities, leveraging demographic or inherited attributes for structured identification.
